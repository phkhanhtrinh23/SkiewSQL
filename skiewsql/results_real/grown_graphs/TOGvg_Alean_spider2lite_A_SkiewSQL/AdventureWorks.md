_aggregates individual sales transactions to compute the total revenue generated by each salesperson for each calendar year_

```sql
SELECT sp.businessentityid AS salesperson, strftime('%Y', soh.orderdate) AS year, SUM(sod.orderqty * sod.unitprice * (1 - sod.unitpricediscount)) AS total_sales
FROM salesperson sp
JOIN salesorderheader soh ON sp.businessentityid = soh.salespersonid
JOIN salesorderdetail sod ON soh.salesorderid = sod.salesorderid
GROUP BY sp.businessentityid, strftime('%Y', soh.orderdate)
```

_joins the annual sales totals with the corresponding annual sales quotas per salesperson and year to compute the performance difference_

```sql
SELECT s.salesperson, s.year, s.total_sales, q.SalesQuota AS sales_quota, s.total_sales - q.SalesQuota AS sales_vs_quota_difference FROM salesperson_annual_sales s JOIN SalesPersonQuotaHistory q ON s.salesperson = q.BusinessEntityID AND s.year = CAST(strftime('%Y', q.QuotaDate) AS INTEGER)
```
