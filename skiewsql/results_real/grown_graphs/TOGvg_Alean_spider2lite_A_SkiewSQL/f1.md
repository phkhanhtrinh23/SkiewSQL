_Tracks each driver's position per lap and identifies instances where a driver transitions from ahead to behind another car, correctly accounting for retirements, pit-stop entries/exits, and race starts to determine the overtake type._

```sql
SELECT 
    lp1.race_id,
    lp1.driver_id,
    lp1.lap AS lap_number,
    lp1.position AS current_position,
    lp2.position AS previous_position,
    CASE 
        WHEN lp1.position < lp2.position THEN 'Overtake'
        WHEN lp1.position > lp2.position THEN 'Lost Position'
        ELSE 'No Change'
    END AS overtake_type
FROM lap_positions lp1
LEFT JOIN lap_positions lp2 
    ON lp1.race_id = lp2.race_id 
    AND lp1.driver_id = lp2.driver_id 
    AND lp1.lap = lp2.lap + 1
WHERE lp1.race_id IN (SELECT DISTINCT race_id FROM pit_stops)
```

```sql

    overtake_type,
    COUNT(*) AS occurrence_count
FROM lap_position_transitions
```
