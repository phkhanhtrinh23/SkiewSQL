_one row per lap where a driver transitions from ahead to behind another car in races with available pit stop data, including the derived overtake event type_

```sql
SELECT 
    p2.race_id AS race,
    p2.lap AS lap,
    p1.driver_id AS driver,
    q1.driver_id AS target_driver,
    'Overtake' AS overtake_type
FROM lap_positions p1
JOIN lap_positions p2 ON p1.race_id = p2.race_id AND p1.driver_id = p2.driver_id AND p1.lap = p2.lap - 1
JOIN lap_positions q1 ON p1.race_id = q1.race_id AND p1.lap = q1.lap
JOIN lap_positions q2 ON q1.race_id = q2.race_id AND q1.driver_id = q2.driver_id AND q1.lap = q2.lap - 1
WHERE p1.race_id IN (SELECT DISTINCT race_id FROM pit_stops)
  AND p1.driver_id != q1.driver_id
  AND p1.position < q1.position
  AND p2.position > q2.position
```
