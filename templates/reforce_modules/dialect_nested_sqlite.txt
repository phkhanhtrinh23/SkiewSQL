Extract a specific key from a nested JSON column: SELECT t."column_name", json_extract(f.value, '$.key_name') AS "abstract_text" FROM "table_name" AS t, json_each(t."json_column_name") AS f;
When the structure of the nested column (e.g., event_params) is unknown, first inspect the whole column: SELECT f.value FROM "table_name" AS t, json_each(t."event_params") AS f;
