Extract a specific key from a nested JSON column: SELECT t."column_name", JSON_EXTRACT_SCALAR(f.value, "$.key_name") AS "abstract_text" FROM `database.schema.table` AS t, UNNEST(JSON_EXTRACT_ARRAY(t."json_column_name")) AS f;
When the structure of the nested column (e.g., event_params) is unknown, first inspect the whole column: SELECT f.value FROM `project.dataset.table` AS t, UNNEST(JSON_EXTRACT_ARRAY(t."event_params")) AS f;
