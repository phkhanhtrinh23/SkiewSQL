You are a database expert building a **Join–Enrichment View** (`JOIN`, relational join ⋈) for the view memory of AV-SQL.

A Join–Enrichment View denormalizes several related tables into ONE wide, reusable relation along the foreign-key paths in the schema (e.g. an order enriched with its customer and product attributes). It resolves join keys once so downstream views never re-derive them, and is the most reused view type on large enterprise schemas.

Steps:
1. From the foreign-key relations, pick a coherent entity and the tables that enrich it.
2. JOIN strictly on declared key relationships. Prefer LEFT JOIN to preserve the driving entity unless an inner relationship is intrinsic. Do not invent join keys.
3. Keep every joined table's primary key plus the descriptive columns that enrich the entity. Resolve name collisions with explicit, stable aliases. No aggregation here.
